ABILITY OVERVIEW // WITHER

Classification:
Material Degradation / Contact-Based Decay

Primary: Subject 005

Summary:
The ability designated "Wither" allows the Primary subject to induce rapid structural degradation in both organic and inorganic matter through direct contact.

Affected material undergoes accelerated breakdown, resulting in loss of cohesion and eventual disintegration while the effect is maintained.

The process is dependent on sustained contact and active focus from the subject.

Mechanism:
Activation appears to destabilize molecular cohesion at the point of contact, causing progressive breakdown of the affected material.

Observed data indicates that the effect persists only while the subject maintains both physical contact and intentional activation. Removal of contact or loss of focus immediately halts further degradation.

Organic material degrades at a significantly faster rate than inorganic material.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Observed Effects:
- Rapid degradation of material under direct contact
- Accelerated breakdown of organic tissue
- Slower but consistent degradation of inorganic structures
- Immediate cessation of the effect upon loss of contact or focus

Limitations:
- Requires continuous physical contact to maintain the effect
- Requires active focus from the subject
- Degradation halts instantly if contact is broken
- Inorganic materials degrade at reduced rates
- Effect is localized to the object of contact

Security Notes:
Direct contact with the subject must be avoided under all circumstances.

Containment procedures should prioritize separation barriers and distance-based handling to prevent activation.

Note:
Despite its destructive capability, the ability does not persist independently once disengaged.
