OVERVIEW
This document catalogs observed physical manifestations
associated with avian ability acquisition. Each ability
correlates with distinct morphological adaptations and
visual identifiers, typically emerging during the same
developmental phase as wing formation.

These traits appear consistent across subjects
possessing the same ability, suggesting a standardized
biological response rather than individual mutation.

---

ABSORPTION
Color Association: RED

Observed Manifestations:
- Distal digits darken and harden, forming clawed
  structures
- Ocular formations present on each palm

Notes:
Palm-based ocular structures are fully functional and
provide additional visual input from non-standard
angles. These organs appear to operate independently
of primary vision and are often oriented toward points
of contact during ability use.

---

EMPATHY
Color Association: WHITE

Observed Manifestations:
- Development of a feathered avian tail
- Wing-like structures positioned along the sides of the head (aural region)
- Feather distribution across facial structure, primarily along the cheeks

Notes:
Aural region wing structures are capable of independent movement. 

---

HEALING
Color Association: YELLOW

Observed Manifestations:
- Ocular formations present on the back of each hand

Notes:
Dorsal hand ocular structures are capable of active visual tracking. During healing output, subject subconsciously orients these eyes toward the affected target area, suggesting a role in directing or modulating the process.

---

SHIFTING
Color Association: PINK

Observed Manifestations:
- Small wing structures emerging along the upper back, resembling dorsal fins

Notes:
These structures may assist in stabilizing form during transformation processes.

---

SILVER TONGUE
Color Association: ORANGE

Observed Manifestations:
- No consistent external morphological adaptations observed

Notes:
Lack of visible indicators complicates identification of active use.

---

SPEED
Color Association: BLUE

Observed Manifestations:
- Loss of original lower limbs during manifestation phase
- Regrowth of digitigrade, clawed limbs resembling large avian anatomy

Notes:
Structural adaptation is hypothesized to support rapid acceleration and controlled deceleration under extreme velocity conditions.

---

STRENGTH
Color Association: GRAY

Observed Manifestations:
- Development of a secondary pair of upper limbs, anatomically identical to the primary set

Notes:
Additional limbs exhibit equivalent strength output and coordination capability.

---

TELEPATHY
Color Association: GREEN

Observed Manifestations:
- Ocular formation centered on the forehead

Notes:
The central ocular structure is fully functional and provides an expanded forward field of vision. Subjects demonstrate the ability to process input from this organ concurrently with standard visual channels.

---

WIPE
Color Association: BLACK

Observed Manifestations:
- Dual ring structures containing ocular formations suspended above the subject
- Additional ocular formations present along the cheeks

Notes:
All manifested eyes are functional. The ring structures exhibit rotational motion during ability use, allowing continuous visual coverage of the surrounding environment. Eye coloration matches that of the subject.

---

WITHER
Color Association: PURPLE

Observed Manifestations:
- Progressive discoloration of upper limb skin, transitioning from purple to black at the hands
- Clawed digit formation
- Luminescent vascular patterns visible beneath the skin

Notes:
Given that the subject that developed the Wither ability was the first successful manifestation, it is unclear at this time if the above stated vascular patterns are a result of Wither manifestation or the subject's mutations from early stage gene therapy.

---

DIRECTORATE NOTE
Manifestation traits provide reliable visual identification of subject capabilities and should be used for rapid classification during observation and containment procedures.
