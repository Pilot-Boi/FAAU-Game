ABILITY OVERVIEW // SILVER TONGUE

Classification:
Cognitive Influence / Vocal Compulsion

Primary: Subject 002, Subject 007

Summary:
The ability designated "Silver Tongue" allows the Primary subject to induce compelled compliance in a target through direct verbal instruction.

Upon activation, the subject’s speech overrides resistance responses, forcing the target to execute issued commands regardless of prior intent.

Targets remain aware during the effect, but report a loss of control over their own actions.

Mechanism:
Activation requires the target to hear and cognitively process the subject’s voice. The subject must maintain focus on the intended target during instruction.

Observed data suggests the ability suppresses voluntary motor control while preserving conscious awareness, resulting in enforced directive execution.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Observed Effects:
- Immediate compliance with spoken commands
- Preservation of target awareness during execution
- Reported sensation of displaced or suppressed control
- Temporary inability to resist or interrupt actions
- Post-effect cognitive disorientation and delayed response

Limitations:
- Requires clear auditory reception of the command
- Most effective when applied to a single target
- Application to multiple targets significantly reduces stability and induces strain on the subject
- Compulsion effect does not persist beyond completion of the command

Security Notes:
This ability presents a significant risk to personnel and containment protocols.

Direct verbal interaction with the subject should be restricted. Use of audio dampening or communication filters is recommended during handling procedures.

Note:
Affected individuals consistently report the experience as observing their own actions without the ability to intervene.
