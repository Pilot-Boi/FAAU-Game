ABILITY OVERVIEW // SPEED

Classification:
Kinetic Enhancement / Accelerated Locomotion

Primary: Subject 002, Subject 006

Summary:
The ability designated "Speed" allows the Primary subject to achieve extreme acceleration and movement velocity across both aerial and terrestrial environments.

The subject is capable of sustained high-speed flight, rapid directional changes, and enhanced striking speed, including physical attacks delivered at accelerated
rates.

Mechanism:
Activation appears to involve full-body kinetic enhancement, increasing muscular output, reaction speed, and neurological processing.

Observed data suggests the subject’s physiology is adapted to withstand the stresses associated with high-velocity movement.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Observed Effects:
- High-speed aerial and ground movement
- Rapid acceleration and deceleration
- Enhanced reaction time and coordination
- Increased force delivery in physical strikes

Limitations:
- Extended use induces physical fatigue
- Precision decreases at higher velocities
- Environmental obstacles increase risk during movement
- Requires spatial awareness to maintain control

Security Notes:
Subjects possessing this ability are capable of rapid repositioning and evasion.

Containment procedures should account for high-speed movement and limited reaction windows.

Note:
Subjects with this ability undergo a concurrent physiological adaptation during manifestation, occurring alongside wing development.
