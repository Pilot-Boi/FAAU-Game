AVIAN ABILITY OVERVIEW

The following document provides a general overview of observed avian abilities. Each entry includes a brief description and reference to associated documentation.

Detailed analysis, testing data, and behavioral impact are maintained in linked files.

--- ABSORPTION ---
Allows the subject to draw in and store base forms of energy through direct contact, and later convert and release that energy.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/absorption.txt

--- EMPATHY ---
Allows the subject to perceive the emotions, physical stimuli, and pain of bonded individuals through emotional connection or direct contact.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/empathy.txt


--- HEALING ---
Allows the subject to accelerate the natural healing process of others through direct contact, marked by a visible energy emission.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/healing.txt


--- SHIFTING ---
Allows the subject to completely alter their physical form to replicate another individual or organism.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/shifting.txt


--- SILVER TONGUE ---
Allows the subject to induce compelled compliance in targets through verbal instruction.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/silver_tongue.txt


--- SPEED ---
Allows the subject to achieve extreme movement velocity across aerial and terrestrial environments.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/speed.txt


--- STRENGTH ---
Allows the subject to exert force significantly above baseline human levels.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/strength.txt


--- TELEPATHY ---
Allows the subject to perceive and influence the thoughts of nearby individuals.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/telepathy.txt


--- WIPE ---
Allows the subject to remove memories from a target through direct cognitive interference.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/wipe.txt


--- WITHER ---
Allows the subject to induce structural degradation in organic and inorganic matter through direct contact.

See: /secure/avians/abilities/wither.txt
