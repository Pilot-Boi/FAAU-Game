ABILITY OVERVIEW // STRENGTH

Classification:
Physical Enhancement / Muscular Augmentation

Primary: Subject 003, Subject 006

Summary:
The ability designated "Strength" allows the Primary subject to exert force significantly above baseline human levels.

Subjects are capable of breaching reinforced materials, including standard concrete barriers, and delivering high-impact physical strikes.

Mechanism:
Activation appears to involve localized muscular amplification, increasing force output without a corresponding increase in overall body mass.

Observed data suggests enhanced force generation is achieved through unknown biological augmentation.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Observed Effects:
- Significant increase in lifting and striking force
- Ability to damage or breach reinforced structures
- Enhanced effectiveness in close-range physical engagement

Limitations:
- Increased strength is not accompanied by increased durability
- Subjects remain vulnerable to injury from their own exertion
- High-force output may result in structural damage to the subject’s own body
- Repeated exertion induces fatigue and strain

Security Notes:
Containment requires reinforced structural materials. Standard holding cells are insufficient for subjects possessing this ability.

Personnel should avoid close-range engagement.

Note:
Subjects with this ability undergo a concurrent physiological adaptation during manifestation, occurring alongside wing development.
