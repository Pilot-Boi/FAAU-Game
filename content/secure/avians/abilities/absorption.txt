ABILITY OVERVIEW // ABSORPTION

Classification:
Energy Intake / Conversion / Release

Primary: Subject 007

Summary:
The ability designated "Absorption" allows the Primary subject to draw in and store base forms of energy through direct contact.

Absorbed energy may be retained and later expelled in a different base form, enabling conversion between energy types.

The process requires physical contact for intake and active control for release.

Mechanism:
Activation appears to involve the capture and temporary containment of external energy sources, followed by conversion into a usable output form.

Observed data suggests the subject is capable of reconfiguring absorbed energy prior to release, allowing for selective output types.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Observed Effects:
- Absorption of energy through direct contact
- Temporary storage of absorbed energy
- Conversion between base energy forms
- Controlled release of stored energy

Limitations:
- Requires direct contact to absorb energy
- Only base energy forms can be absorbed and converted
- Storage capacity appears finite
- Excess intake may result in instability or uncontrolled discharge
- Release requires active intent and control

Security Notes:
Subjects possessing this ability may draw energy from environmental sources or other systems.

Containment protocols should limit access to potential energy sources, including electrical systems and open flame.

Note:
Subject demonstrates a tendency to favor specific output forms during release, specifically flame.
