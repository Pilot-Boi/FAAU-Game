Salem’s eyes turn as cold and harsh as a winter’s storm. She slithers towards you, and the light almost seems to draw away from her. The shadows cast across her face catch on her cheekbones, turning her pale face into the visage of death.

“I’ve had just about enough of you,” she says, and her voice is whisper soft as it carries through the room. “The gods slaughter billions , and you dare to compare a few measly lives to their death count?”

“When they left it broke the world, and our species still hasn’t recovered all the knowledge lost in the breaking,” she hisses, eyes narrowing as she continues towards you. “Centuries of knowledge gone forever, because two beings were slighted. And you DARE to claim that I am no better than them?!”

“I admit my own faults,” Salem snarls. Your back hits a wall, and she looms over you, casting your face in shadows. “I never claimed to be better than the gods…” Her eyes seem to glow like coals. “...But I will be.”

She turns away from you, and you breathe a shaky breath. “You are no friend of mine,” she hisses, facing away. “But if you continue making these foolish accusations, I’ll tear out your throat myself.”
